# Patent non-assertion covenant

The Kindred Agency Inc. ("Kindred") makes the following irrevocable commitment
in respect of the Agent Identity Card and Agent Delegation Credential
specifications published in `spec/`, and the reference implementation published
in this repository.

**Kindred will not assert any patent it owns or controls, now or in future,
against any person or organisation for implementing, using, deploying,
distributing, or creating derivative works of these specifications or this
reference implementation.**

This covenant:

- runs with the specifications and the implementation, and binds any successor,
  assignee, or acquirer of the relevant patents;
- is not conditional on membership of any programme, purchase of any product,
  or any commercial relationship with Kindred;
- survives the expiry or termination of any agreement between Kindred and any
  implementer;
- is in addition to, and does not narrow, the patent grant in section 3 of the
  Apache License 2.0 that governs the reference implementation.

The only reservation: this covenant does not extend to a party that first
asserts a patent against Kindred, or against any other implementer, in respect
of these specifications. That is a defensive reservation and is the standard
reciprocity condition; it exists so the covenant cannot be used as cover for an
attack on the people relying on it.

Kindred's intent in publishing this is that no implementer of these
specifications should have to consider Kindred's patent position at all.
